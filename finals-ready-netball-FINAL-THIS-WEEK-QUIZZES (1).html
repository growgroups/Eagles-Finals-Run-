Our five rules

Our five calls

Centre-pass principles

1
ONE - WA first
WA attacks. GA holds, then becomes second option. C reads WA, GA, then RESET.
2
TWO - GA first
GA attacks first. WA holds width, then reoffers. Do not move together.
3
SPLIT
Create separation. One threatens short, one gives depth. C reads the defender.
4
CLEAR
First movement moves a defender. Second movement attacks the space left behind.

Throw-ins

Every throw-in: forward option · second option · safe RESET.

Sideline: create width and depth. Do not all run at the thrower.

Attacking baseline: shooter hold, moving option, safety option. If nothing opens, reset and rebuild.

Defensive baseline: secure the first pass, give width, then connect through court.

Feed the shooters

Circle picture: GS, GA and feeder create separate angles, never one line.

GS holds: maintain depth and make the defender choose. GA moves: attack top or side space. Feeder: see front, baseline and reset.

One shooter holds while one moves. If both are covered, RESET rather than forcing a hope ball.

BOX defence + keepers

Closest defender: controlled pressure on the ball. Everyone else: protect the next pass and dangerous space.

GK: PROTECT · TALK · START. GD: pressure circle entry, connect with GK, then become an attacker immediately after possession changes.

The box moves when the ball moves. No ball watching. No desperate lunges that break our shape.

Transition

WIN → SECURE → EYES UP → SPREAD → GO

First safe pass. Midcourt connects. WA/GA get ahead. GS gives depth. When possession is lost: PRESS - nearest player pressures and everyone else finds a job.

Game day

Arrive 45 minutes before the game. Be organised early so the last 30 minutes are about the team, warm-up and competing.

ARRIVE READY → PHONE AWAY → TEAM SHEET → WARM UP → SWITCH ON → GO HARD

45 minutes before - arrive ready

1
Be at the courts 45 minutes before first whistle
Shoes on, uniform ready and hair/jewellery sorted. Do not arrive at the start of warm-up.
2
Toilet before team preparation
Go early. Do not wait until the warm-up has started unless you genuinely need to.
3
Water bottle filled and courtside
Bring your own full bottle. Have it where you can reach it during breaks.
4
Phone away in the bag
Once you arrive, the phone goes away and stays away until after the game. Be present with the team.
5
Sign the team sheet
Complete the required team-sheet check early so nobody is chasing players just before the game.

30 minutes before - warm-up starts

Everyone is ready to move at T-30. No shoes still being tied, no phones, no wandering off.

1
Raise body temperature
Easy movement first, then dynamic mobility and controlled changes of direction.
2
Landing and footwork
Balanced landings, deceleration, pivots and sharp feet before full-speed court work.
3
Ball work
Sharp hands, accurate passing, leads, reoffers and communication. Build speed and intensity.
4
Position and unit connections
Shooters finish with feeds and shots. Defenders connect and talk. Midcourt checks timing and first options.

Before the team talk - know your job

Every player should already know the game plans. Game day is for reminders and reads, not learning a play for the first time.

KNOW THE CALL → KNOW YOUR START → KNOW YOUR FIRST MOVE → KNOW THE RESET

Starting seven and bench: everyone stays switched on. Know the opposition match-up, watch your position, listen to instructions and be ready to enter immediately.

Final 5 minutes - compete

Talk positively. Eyes up. Strong body language. No worrying about court time, mistakes or the scoreboard before the game begins.

First job: know the first thing your position must do well.

After any mistake: NEXT BALL.

Game-day bag check

Uniform · netball shoes · socks · full water bottle · simple familiar snack · any required personal medication managed with parent/carer · hair ties · weather layer/sunscreen as needed.

Before leaving home: eat a familiar meal, hydrate, check game time and court, and make sure the whole kit is packed.

Our commitment

We arrive prepared. We know the plans. We warm up properly. We support every teammate. We play hard, smart and together. When something goes wrong, we respond with the next ball.

Finals ready

Arrive fresh, fuelled and calm. Recovery is part of training.

🍌 Fuel

Night before: normal balanced dinner with familiar carbohydrate foods such as pasta, rice, potatoes or bread, plus normal protein, vegetables and water.

Game morning: familiar breakfast such as cereal and milk, toast and eggs, porridge, yoghurt and fruit, or toast with banana.

Easy snacks: banana, fruit, yoghurt, toast or a simple sandwich. Bananas provide carbohydrate and potassium and can be a convenient familiar game-day snack.

Avoid experimenting with new foods on game day. Energy drinks are not part of this plan.

💧 Hydrate

Do not start the game thirsty. Drink regularly through the day, bring a full bottle and drink during breaks. After heavy sweating, fluids plus normal meals help replace fluid and sodium.

🧘 Recover + soreness

COOL → DRINK → EAT → SLEEP

After training and games: 5-10 minutes easy movement, gentle mobility, normal food, water and good sleep. Friday is for freshness, not extra hard conditioning.

Normal post-training soreness is different from injury. Significant pain, swelling, bruising, limping, instability or loss of function should be reported rather than treated as routine soreness.

🩹 Injury awareness

STOP → TELL → ASSESS → RECOVER

If something hurts or does not feel right: stop the activity and tell the coach or parent. Do not hide an injury or try to prove you can play through significant pain.

Watch for: sudden or increasing pain, swelling, bruising, limping, reduced movement, weakness, instability, or difficulty putting weight through a limb.

Return to play: an injured player should be properly assessed and should not return just because it is an important finals game. Follow the advice of the parent and appropriate qualified health professional.

Head impact or suspected concussion: come off and report symptoms immediately. Do not return to play that day. Follow the applicable youth concussion protocol and obtain appropriate healthcare guidance before returning to sport.

😴 Sleep

Pack gear early. Wind down Friday night. Good sleep helps the body recover and helps decision making on Saturday.

🧠 Mental ready

BREATHE → ROLE → FIRST JOB → TEAM

Mistake routine: MISTAKE → RESET → NEXT BALL. No blame. No negative body language. Do the next job.

Bench: watch your opponent and position, listen, support teammates and be ready to enter with a clear job.

Finals pressure: when ahead, value possession. When behind, do not chase the scoreboard in one pass. Win the next ball.
