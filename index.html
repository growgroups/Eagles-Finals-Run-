Game Plans

Every play has a clear purpose. Read the defence. Never force a covered pass.

A3 - Corner Stack: Defender Up

STACK HOLDS -> DEFENDER UP -> GS OUT -> GA PUNCH -> GOAL
  1. C starts in the centre circle.
  2. WA and GA hold the stack and occupy the normal centre-pass defenders.
  3. Attacking WD or GD drives forward into the centre third after the whistle.
  4. Pass 1: C to WD/GD.
  5. GS drives out of the goal circle to link.
  6. GA releases from the stack and punches hard into the goal circle.
  7. Pass 2: WD/GD to GS.
  8. Pass 3: GS to GA inside the circle.
  9. GA turns to goal. GS follows for rebound.

Rule: if the defender-up option is denied, use the normal WA/GA option. Do not force it.

Centre Pass Rules

Centre-pass players shown on transverse lines start in legal areas before the whistle. Timing creates space. The first receiver is only the start of the play.

Game Day

ARRIVE READY -> PHONE AWAY -> TEAM SHEET -> WARM UP -> SWITCH ON -> GO HARD
  • 45 minutes before: arrive at the courts.
  • Go to the toilet before team preparation starts.
  • Uniform, shoes, socks and hair ready.
  • Water bottle filled and courtside.
  • Phone away in your bag until after the game.
  • Team sheet completed early.
  • 30 minutes before: everyone ready to start the team warm-up.
  • Know the game plans, starting position and first movement.
  • Know your match-up and positional job.
  • Bench players stay involved and ready to enter immediately.
  • Bring familiar food and snacks. Banana or other normal carbohydrate snack can be useful before play.
  • Report pain, swelling, bruising, limping or injury concerns. Do not hide an injury.
  • After mistakes: NEXT BALL. Reset immediately.
  • Go hard, play for each other and give everything.
